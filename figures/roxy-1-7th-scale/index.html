As a big fan of Mushoku Tensei, there is no better character to commit the sin of idolatry for than Rudeus' God, Roxy Migurdia.

The overall quality of the figure is quite good. The hair, face, and clothes are all detailed enough to match the size of the figure, while not being too fleshed out as to overshadow the simple color palette and Roxy's somewhat rustic charm.

The cape in particular, which is just a giant sheet of plastic, doesn't have the sharp edges I have seen on other scale figures with large billowing clothing, which seems like a small ask but does a lot to impact her overall appearance.

The stand is also well made. This is one of the few figures I own whose stand is more than just a transparent plastic circle, and while a few stone steps is not exactly a mind-bogglingly complex concept, the impliciation of her walking down the steps adds a degree of motion that figures without unique stands lack.
